    Approach

    How I work

    Individualized, whole-person treatment plans that restore the body's natural ability to heal — combining rigorous testing with the widest toolkit in integrative GI medicine.

    Investigate deeply

    Advanced stool testing, breath testing, and functional labs to find the actual driver of your symptoms — not just a label for them.

    Treat the whole system

    Western and Chinese herbal medicine, nutritional therapy, acupuncture, neural therapy, and injection therapies — alongside, not instead of, conventional care when it's needed.

    Partner with your GI team

    I know the pharmacology of IBD as well as the natural medicine. I work with your gastroenterologist, and I teach other physicians how to do the same.

    Getting Started

    What to expect

    1

    Schedule your first visit

    Visits happen at Open Wellness PDX in Portland, Oregon, or virtually by telehealth. Call the clinic or book through the patient portal.

    2

    A thorough intake

    We go through your full history — every symptom, medication, surgery, and prior test. Complex GI cases deserve more than a fifteen-minute appointment.

    3

    Testing and a plan

    Targeted functional testing where it changes the treatment, then a written, staged plan — with clear expectations about timeline and follow-up.

    4

    Ongoing partnership

    Chronic digestive disease isn't fixed in one visit. We adjust the plan as your gut responds, and I stay in your corner for the long haul.
