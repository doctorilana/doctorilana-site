Microbiome

Biofilms, Parasites & Dysbiosis

What It Is

Gut microbes rarely live as free-floating cells — they build biofilms, structured communities wrapped in a protective matrix that shields them from antimicrobials and your immune system. Biofilms are a major reason chronic gut infections relapse after 'successful' treatment. Add parasites and broader dysbiosis, and you have an ecosystem problem, not a single-bug problem.

Common Signs
  • Infections that keep coming back
  • Partial response to antimicrobials
  • Chronic loose stools or gas
  • Travel history with lingering symptoms
  • Multiple failed protocols
How I Approach It

I'm known for biofilm work — I've taught it on podcasts across the integrative GI world. High-quality stool testing, biofilm-disrupting agents sequenced with antimicrobials, and rebuilding a resilient ecosystem afterward so the problem doesn't return.
