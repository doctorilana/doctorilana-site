For Companies

Clinical depth for companies in gut health

I advise supplement, diagnostic, and functional-medicine companies as a whole-systems microbiome advisor — bringing 18 years of frontline GI practice to product strategy, physician education, and clinical credibility.

Services

How I work with companies

01

Scientific & clinical advisory

Advisory board service and ongoing counsel for companies building products in the GI and microbiome space — grounded in what actually happens in clinic, not just what's in the literature.

02

Physician & practitioner education

Webinars, training curricula, and educational content that teach clinicians how to use your product or test well. I've served as a physician health educator for Nestlé Health Science and an educator-partner with Diagnostic Solutions Lab on the GI-MAP.

03

Product & formulation input

Clinical review of formulations, dosing, and positioning for supplement and therapeutic products — what a GI-focused clinician would actually recommend, and why.

04

Diagnostic & lab consulting

Interpretation frameworks, clinical validation perspective, and practitioner-facing guidance for stool testing, breath testing, and emerging GI diagnostics.

05

Content & thought leadership

Podcast appearances, co-branded educational content, and speaking that reaches both practitioners and patients — through my own shows and a decade of guest expertise across the integrative-health podcast world.

06

Key opinion leader partnerships

Long-term KOL relationships for brands that want a credentialed, board-certified voice in naturopathic gastroenterology — not a spokesperson, a clinician.

Why It Works

Advice from the exam room, not the sidelines

I still see complex GI patients every week. That means the guidance you get reflects how products, tests, and protocols perform in real clinical practice — with real patients, real comorbidities, and real insurance constraints.
