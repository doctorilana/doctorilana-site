Mentorship

Case consults & mentoring

Sometimes a course isn't enough — you need another set of eyes on the case in front of you.

One-on-one case consultation

Bring me your most difficult GI cases — IBD that won't respond, SIBO that keeps relapsing, the patient whose workup makes no sense. We'll go through the case together: history, labs, differentials, and a staged treatment strategy you can implement.

Clinical mentorship

Ongoing mentorship for residents and practicing clinicians building a GI-focused practice — from test interpretation and therapeutic sequencing to the business realities of specialty practice. I supervise residents and consult with physicians across the country.
